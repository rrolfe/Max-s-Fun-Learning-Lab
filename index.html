Grown-up corner

Progress and language are saved in this browser on this device. No account or cloud sync. Optional videos load from external providers when you tap Play. Read-aloud uses a voice for the selected language from your device.

Adding something new

The included START-HERE guide explains how to upload and update the site. English lessons are in the data folder. Spanish lessons and the language controls are bundled in app.js. Ask for both languages when adding a new lesson.

Family stories and photos

Family connections were supplied by Max's family. Place photos show destinations, not family members. When published on GitHub Pages, these family connections and files can be read publicly.

Each new correct answer, found word, hidden object, or completed drawing earns 1 point. A 10-word search earns 10 points. Previously earned points are kept. Replays are welcome practice, but the same solved item earns points only once.

Baseball costs 10 earned points for three innings. Spending points never lowers the lifetime learning score. Unfinished baseball games resume without another charge, including after switching languages. Scores and game progress stay on this device.
